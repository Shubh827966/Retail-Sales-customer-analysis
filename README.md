# Retail-Sales-customer-analysis
A customer-level table for drilling down to individual orders A few things I found:→ Electronics brings in far more revenue than any other category, even though order counts across categories are almost the same.   So the difference is order value, not volume. → Revenue peaked at 65M in 2023 and dropped to 51M in 2024.   Around 15% of orders Return
I started with the question: 

Where is the money coming from?
Which orders are stuck? 
How bad are the returns? 

What it covers: Revenue, order status, returns and pending orders in one executive view Revenue by region, product category and year Slicers for payment method (UPI, credit card, COD, EMI, net banking) and product category

A customer-level table for drilling down to individual orders A few things I found:

→ Electronics brings in far more revenue than any other category, even though order counts across categories are almost the same. 

So the difference is order value, not volume. → Revenue peaked at 65M in 2023 and dropped to 51M in 2024.

→ Around 15% of orders were returned.

which is something a business would want to dig into. Built with Power BI and DAX [add SQL / Excel / Python if you used them for cleaning].
